---
title: "What bunny can do"
description: "The abilities bunny reaches for on its own, from live web search and travel picks to documents, reminders, and shareable lists."
---

bunny has a set of abilities it uses without being asked. There is nothing to switch on and nothing to configure. Just say what you want in plain language, and bunny picks the right ability for the job and folds the result into its reply.

## The abilities

| Ask for this | What bunny does |
| --- | --- |
| "what's happening with the election" | Searches the live web for news, prices, and facts from today. Every search is saved to your **Web Search** history in the Mini App. |
| "read this and tell me the gist" plus a link | Opens the page you pasted and answers from what is actually on it. |
| "best ramen near me", "hotels in Lisbon in May", "flights to Tokyo" | Searches live places, restaurants, hotels, and flights, with real ratings and prices. Three or more picks turn into a shareable list page. Flights link out to Google Flights. |
| "make me a PDF of that" or "write it up as a Word doc" | Builds the document and sends it straight into the chat as a Telegram attachment. |
| "remind me to call mom on Sunday" | Adds it to your bunny calendar as an event, reminder, or automation. See [Calendar and automations](/user/chat/calendar-and-automations). |
| "remember that I'm allergic to shellfish" | Saves the fact for good, so you never have to repeat it. See [Memory](/user/chat/memory). |
| "what's in my inbox today" | Reads your connected Gmail and Google Calendar, if you have hooked them up. See [Connected apps](/user/chat/connected-apps). |
| "what's 18% of 4,320" | Works the numbers out properly instead of guessing. |

## Shareable lists

When bunny recommends three or more places, restaurants, hotels, or flights, it does not dump them into the chat. It builds a proper web page and sends you one line plus the link.

The page is public, so you can forward it to anyone. They do not need Telegram or a bunnyOS account to open it. It is handy for planning a trip with friends, sending dinner options to the group, or keeping a shortlist you can come back to.

Every list you make is also collected under **Lists** in the Mini App, so nothing gets lost in your chat history.

## Sharing your location

For anything "near me", bunny needs to know where you are. It sends you a one-tap **share location** button, you tap it, and the search finishes on its own.

Your spot is saved, so "near me" keeps working without you sharing it every time. If it gets old, bunny asks for a fresh one. Location sharing works in private chats only, so it never happens in a group.

<Note>
If a search was waiting on your location, bunny completes it the moment you share, usually with the list link right there in the reply.
</Note>
