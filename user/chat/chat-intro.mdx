Talking to bunny is just texting. There is no prompt to get right and no special syntax to learn. Open the chat, say what you want, and bunny answers.

## Just text it like a friend

bunny writes back the way a friend would, casual, lowercase, and short. Two or three sentences beats a paragraph, so you get the answer instead of an essay.

When there is more to show, bunny can send a table or hide a spoiler behind a tap to reveal. Times arrive as tappable timestamps shown in your own local time.

## While it works

bunny gives you a running read on what it is doing, so you are never left staring at a silent chat.

| Signal | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| 👀 reaction on your message | bunny has seen it and started |
| Typing indicator | bunny is working on the answer |
| Gray status bubble | What bunny is doing right now, with a live timer counting the seconds |
| ⚡ reaction on your message | The answer has landed |

The status bubble appears in private chats only, and only if the answer takes more than a moment. It names the step (searching the web, checking the calendar, writing a document) and swaps to the answer text as soon as bunny starts writing.

## Sending photos

Send a photo and bunny looks at it. Add a caption to say what you want to know ("what plant is this", "translate this menu"), or send it with no caption at all and bunny will just tell you what it sees.

Photos can be up to 10 MB. They work in [group chats](/user/chat/group-chats) too.

## Fresh starts

Send `/reset` to wipe the conversation history for that chat. bunny forgets what you were just talking about and starts clean. That is useful when you switch topics and the old conversation is getting in the way.

`/reset` only touches the chat you send it in, so resetting a group leaves your private chat untouched. Your long-term memory survives a reset. See [Memory](/user/chat/memory) for how to view or clear that.

<Note>
  bunny handles text, photos, and shared locations today. Voice notes, videos, stickers, and file uploads are not supported yet.
</Note>
