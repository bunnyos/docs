title: "bunny in group chats"
description: "Add bunny to a Telegram group, learn the four ways to summon it, and catch up on the conversation you missed."
---

bunny works in group chats as well as private ones. Add it to any Telegram group and it sits quietly in the background. It does not chime in on your conversation, react to things, or answer questions nobody asked it. It waits until someone summons it.

## The four ways to summon bunny

Any of these gets bunny's attention. Use whichever is quickest in the moment.

| How | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| @mention it | `@bunnyagentbot what time does the museum close` |
| Reply to one of its messages | Tap reply on a bunny message and keep going |
| Start with `/bunny` | `/bunny find us a dinner spot near the station` |
| Open with "hey bunny" | `hey bunny, what's the weather in lisbon` |

Photos work in groups too. Send the photo and summon bunny in the caption, and it will look at the image the same way it does in a private chat.

## What bunny keeps while it is in the group

To make catch-up possible, bunny keeps a short rolling log of recent messages in the group it is in. Only the most recent messages are kept, older ones fall off as new ones arrive.

Remove bunny from the group and the logging stops.

<Note>
  If bunny has not been keeping a log, it will say so rather than guess. Catch-up only covers the time bunny has actually been sitting in the group.
</Note>

## What stays separate

Groups and private chats are kept apart on purpose.

- **Conversation history is per group.** What the group has been talking to bunny about is separate from your private chat, and separate from every other group. Sending `/reset` in a group clears that group's thread and leaves your private chat alone.
- **Long-term memory stays personal.** The facts bunny remembers about you are yours. Being in a group with someone does not give them access to your memory, and bunny does not build a shared memory of the group. See [Memory](/user/chat/memory).
- **bunny answers one message at a time per chat.** In a busy group, wait for the answer before summoning it again.
