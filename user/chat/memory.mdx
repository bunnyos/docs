bunny keeps a long-term memory of you. Not a transcript of everything you have ever typed, just the durable facts that make you you. It builds that memory quietly as you chat and draws on it in every reply, so you never have to reintroduce yourself.

## What bunny remembers

bunny saves the kind of details a thoughtful friend would hold on to.

- Your name, city, and timezone
- Your job, employer, and the projects you are working on
- People in your life, partners, kids, colleagues, and pets
- Preferences, likes and dislikes, interests and hobbies
- Health and fitness details
- Goals and plans
- Upcoming events like trips, deadlines, and birthdays
- The tools and services you use

Small things count. Your dog's name, the fact that you hate mornings, that you are learning Spanish. Those are exactly what bunny keeps.

## How facts get saved

Two ways, and both are effortless.

**Automatically.** After each exchange, bunny looks back over what you said and pulls out anything worth keeping. If a new fact contradicts an old one, the new one replaces it, so moving city or changing jobs updates cleanly.

**On request.** Say "remember this" (or "remember that I am allergic to shellfish") and bunny saves it right away, no waiting.

## What bunny does not save

One-off questions, whatever task you happen to be doing right now, general knowledge, and chit-chat about bunny itself all pass straight through. Asking for the weather in Lisbon does not become a permanent fact about you. Mentioning that you are moving to Lisbon does.

## Seeing and clearing your memory

Three commands do three different things, and the difference matters.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/memory` | Lists the facts bunny has saved about you |
| `/forget` | Erases everything bunny remembers about you, permanently |
| `/reset` | Clears only the current conversation thread, memory stays intact |

<Note>
`/reset` is the gentle one. bunny still knows everything it has learned about you afterwards. Use `/forget` when you want the long-term memory itself gone.
</Note>

If bunny has nothing saved yet, `/memory` replies with "i got nothing saved about you yet. tell me something worth remembering".

## The Memory screen in the Mini App

For a closer look, open the [Mini App](/user/getting-started/mini-app) with `/app` and tap **Memory** under **Services**. There you can do things chat cannot.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Search your facts">
    Type in the **Search** field to filter down to the facts you care about. Each one shows its category above the text.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete a single fact">
    Tap the trash icon next to a fact. A **Forget this fact?** dialog quotes it back to you, then tap **Forget** to remove just that one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear everything">
    Tap the red **Clear All Memories** button, then confirm with **Clear All**. This is the same as sending `/forget` in chat.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
Clearing all memories makes bunny forget everything it has learned about you, and it cannot be undone. There is no way to restore the facts afterwards.
</Warning>
