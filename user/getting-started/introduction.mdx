bunnyOS is a decentralized network built around **small language models**. These are AI models compact enough to run on everyday hardware. They can still reason, use tools, and get real work done.

The answers are served by **bunnyCompute**, a network of machines supplied by people all over the world. Instead of routing every request to a data center, bunnyOS spreads the work across ordinary computers that people already own. Those people earn Carrots for every job their machine handles.

The flagship product built on that network is **bunny**, a personal AI agent that lives inside Telegram. Open [@bunnyagentbot](https://t.me/bunnyagentbot), send a message, and bunny answers.

## Why small models

A small model is light enough to run on a gaming GPU or an Apple Silicon laptop. That one fact changes everything about who can serve it. A frontier model needs racks of specialized hardware in a data center, so a handful of companies decide what it costs and who gets access. A small model does not.

Because the model is cheap and fast to run, the cost per answer stays low enough that everyone can chat for close to free. Machines that were sitting idle become Carrots for the people who supply them. Small is what makes both sides of that trade possible.

## What bunny can do

bunny is a full agent, not a chat box. Here is the short version, with the details on their own pages.

- **Chat, and remember you.** Talk to bunny like a friend who gets things done, and it saves the things worth keeping.
- **Search the live web.** News, prices, facts from this morning, and full pages you paste in.
- **Find places and plan travel.** Places, restaurants, hotels, and flights from live data, and longer lists of picks become a page you can share with anyone.
- **Write documents.** Ask for a PDF or a Word document and bunny sends the file straight into the chat.
- **Keep your calendar.** Events, reminders that ping you, and automations that run a task and message you the result.
- **Gmail and Google Calendar integration.** Connect them once and bunny can search and summarize what is in your inbox and your calendar.
- **Join your group chats.** Add bunny to a group and it answers when you summon it.

## What Carrots are for

Chatting runs on **free usage** that refills every day, so most people never think about cost. When usage runs out, answers spend **Carrots**, the backup credit of the bunnyCompute network. New accounts start with a grant of them, and you can earn more by supplying compute or staking `$OS`. See [Carrots and OS explained](/user/carrots/explained).
