Getting started with bunny takes about ten seconds. There is nothing to install, no account to create, and no password to remember. Your Telegram account is your identity, so if you already have Telegram, you already have everything you need.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open bunny in Telegram">
    Tap [https://t.me/bunnyagentbot](https://t.me/bunnyagentbot) and Telegram will open a chat with bunny.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send /start">
    bunny greets you by name and gives you a short tour of what it can do. The message comes with an **Open bunnyOS app** button that takes you straight into the [Mini App](/user/getting-started/mini-app).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Just text it">
    That is the whole setup. Type a question, a half-formed idea, or a task, and bunny answers. No menus to learn first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
You can add bunny to a group chat too, where it stays quiet until you summon it. See [bunny in group chats](/user/chat/group-chats).
</Tip>

## Commands

Most of the time you will just talk to bunny in plain language. These commands are there when you want to go somewhere directly.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/help` | Shows the intro and this list of commands |
| `/app` | Opens the bunnyOS Mini App |
| `/reset` | Starts a fresh conversation |
| `/memory` | Shows what bunny remembers about you |
| `/forget` | Erases bunny's memory of you |
| `/carrots` | Shows your bunnyCompute network balance |
| `/subscribe` | Gets you bunny pro and its monthly usage |
| `/connect` | Connects Gmail or Google Calendar |
| `/connections` | Shows your connected apps |
| `/disconnect` | Disconnects an app |

<Note>
`/app` and `/subscribe` work in private chats only. Send them in a group and bunny asks you to message it directly instead.
</Note>
