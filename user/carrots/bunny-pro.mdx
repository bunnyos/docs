---
title: "bunny pro"
description: "The optional monthly subscription that gives you much more usage and a bigger, sharper model, paid with Telegram Stars."
---

bunny pro is the paid tier. For `$9` a month you get a generous helping of monthly usage on top of your free usage. Every answer also comes from a bigger, sharper model instead of the free one. Everything happens inside Telegram, with no card to enter and no website to visit.

## What you get

| | Free | bunny pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Model | The standard model | A bigger, sharper model |
| Usage | Free usage that refills daily | Monthly pro usage on top of the free usage |
| Price | Free | `$9` a month |

You do not have to switch anything on. The bigger model is used for every answer while pro is active.

## How to subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Send /subscribe">
    Send `/subscribe` to bunny in a private chat, or open the [Mini App](/user/getting-started/mini-app) and tap the **Get pro for \$9** row under **Manage**. `/subscribe` does not work in group chats, so message bunny directly.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the get pro button">
    bunny replies with a short summary and a button to get pro for `$9` a month. Tapping it opens the Telegram Stars checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the payment">
    Confirm in Telegram and you are done. bunny reacts to the payment and confirms that pro is live.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Payment uses Telegram Stars, Telegram's own in-app currency. Buying Stars and paying for bunny pro both happen inside Telegram.
</Note>

## How pro usage works

While pro is active, your answers draw on the monthly pro usage rather than your Carrot balance. Your earned and granted Carrots sit untouched while it lasts. See [Carrots and OS explained](/user/carrots/explained) for the exact spending order.

Pro usage is a monthly quota, not a top-up of Carrots. It resets at the start of each cycle and whatever you did not use does not carry over. It also stays separate from your balance, so it never mixes with Carrots you earned.

To see how much of it you have used, open the Mini App and tap **Usage** under **Manage**.

## Managing your subscription

bunny pro renews every month automatically. Billing lives entirely with Telegram, so you cancel the same way you cancel any Stars subscription, in Telegram settings under **My Stars**. Send `/subscribe` at any time to see whether you are subscribed and when the current period ends.

If you cancel or the subscription lapses, nothing is lost. You keep chatting on the free model with your free usage and whatever Carrot balance you have, and you can subscribe again later.

<Tip>
If you only chat lightly, you may never need pro. Check **Usage** in the Mini App for a week first, then decide.
</Tip>
