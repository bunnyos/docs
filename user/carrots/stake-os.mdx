Staking locks up your `$OS` on the Base network so it earns Carrots for you. It is the hands-off way to keep your balance topped up, with no trading and nothing to manage day to day. Everything happens on the **Stake \$OS** screen in [the Mini App](/user/getting-started/mini-app).

## What you earn

Staked `$OS` earns Carrots every day. The bigger your stake, the more you earn. The **Carrots per day** row on the staking screen shows exactly what yours brings in.

The Carrots are credited automatically to your bunnyOS account and show up in [your Carrots history](/user/carrots/your-carrots) as **Stake** entries. There is nothing to claim.

## How to stake

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the staking screen">
    Open the [Mini App](/user/getting-started/mini-app) and choose **Stake \$OS** under **Earn Carrots**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet">
    Tap **Connect Wallet** and pick the wallet that holds your `$OS`. bunnyOS staking is on Base.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Link the wallet to your account">
    Your wallet asks you to sign a short message. Signing is free and costs no gas. It tells bunnyOS which account to send the Carrots to, and rewards only flow to a linked wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount and stake">
    Type how much `$OS` you want to stake (or tap **Max**), then tap **Stake** and confirm in your wallet. If your wallet asks for a token approval first, confirm that too, then the stake goes through.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What the screen shows

| Row | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Staked** | How much `$OS` you currently have staked. |
| **Carrots per day** | Your most recent daily reward. |
| **Wallet balance** | The `$OS` sitting in your connected wallet, ready to stake. |
| **Pending unstake** | `$OS` waiting out the unstake cooldown. |
| **Network total staked** | How much everyone has staked, across the whole network. |

<Note>
A brand new stake shows its first reward after the next daily credit, so **Carrots per day** may read `first credit pending` for a while. Nothing is wrong, your Carrots are on their way.
</Note>

## Unstaking

Getting your `$OS` back takes two steps, with a cooldown in between.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request unstake">
    Enter the amount and tap **Request unstake**. The screen then shows a live countdown until the tokens are ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw">
    Once the countdown finishes, tap **Withdraw** to send the `$OS` back to your wallet. Changed your mind? Tap **Cancel** at any point while the request is open and the tokens go straight back to earning.
  </Step>
</Steps>

`$OS` waiting in the cooldown earns nothing, so only request an unstake when you actually want the tokens back.

<Warning>
If you unlink your wallet, your daily Carrots stop until you link a wallet again.
</Warning>
