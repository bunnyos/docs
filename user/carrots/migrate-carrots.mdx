---
title: "Migrate your Carrots"
description: "Move the Carrots from your old bunnyOS account across to Telegram with a single sign-in."
---

If you collected Carrots on the old bunnyOS web app, they do not disappear. You can move that balance across to your Telegram account and keep using it with [bunny](https://t.me/bunnyagentbot). The whole thing takes about a minute.

There is nothing to type, no code to copy, and no wallet to connect. You sign in as your old self, then sign in as your Telegram self, and the two are joined up for you.

<Warning>
You can only do this once per old account, and it cannot be undone. Make sure you are signed in to the right old account and using the right Telegram account before you finish the last step.
</Warning>

## How to migrate

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the migration page">
    Go to [migrate.bunnyos.ai](https://migrate.bunnyos.ai) in any browser. bunny does not link to it from inside Telegram, so this address is the way in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in as your old account">
    Tap **Sign in to migrate your carrots** and sign in with the same email address you used on the old bunnyOS web app. That email is what finds your old balance. Once you are in, your email shows in the top corner so you can confirm you picked the right account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the balance you are about to move">
    The page shows **Your balance** in large orange type. That is the exact amount that will travel with you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with Telegram">
    Under the balance is Telegram's own sign-in button, labeled something like **Log in as Alex**. Tap it. This proves which Telegram account is yours, so nobody else can claim your Carrots.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You are done">
    The page turns into a **Carrots claimed** confirmation showing the claimed amount, your Telegram ID, and the date. Your Carrots are already waiting for you at [@bunnyagentbot](https://t.me/bunnyagentbot).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Where your Carrots show up

Open [@bunnyagentbot](https://t.me/bunnyagentbot) and send `/carrots` to see your new balance. bunny will not message you when the Carrots land, so your balance simply goes up quietly.

For the receipt, send `/app` and open **Carrots Balance** in the Mini App. Your migrated amount appears as an **Adjustment** entry, dated the day you claimed. See [Your Carrots and usage](/user/carrots/your-carrots) for how to read the rest of your history.
