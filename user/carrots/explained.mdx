Most of your chatting runs on **free usage**. **Carrots** are the backup credit you spend when that usage runs out, and **\$OS** is a token you can stake to earn more Carrots. The step-by-step how-tos live on their own pages, linked at the bottom.

## Usage comes first

Every answer bunny gives is generated by a real machine somewhere on the [bunnyCompute](/user/compute/overview) network. You still do not pay to chat, because usage is spent before Carrots ever are.

- **Free usage**, which every account gets and which refills every single day.
- **Pro usage**, a much bigger monthly helping, if you subscribe to [bunny pro](/user/carrots/bunny-pro).

The **Usage** screen in the [Mini App](/user/getting-started/mini-app) shows where you stand as a simple percentage. Because free usage refills tomorrow, light everyday chatting may never cost a single Carrot.

## Carrots, the backup credit

When your usage runs out, answers start spending Carrots from your balance. Each answer costs a small flat number of Carrots. The exact price depends on which model answers, and the bigger bunny pro model costs more per answer than the free one.

Every new account also starts with **a one-time grant of Carrots**, credited automatically the first time you message bunny. It is a cushion for the days your usage runs dry.

## The order things get spent

Every answer draws from the cheapest pocket first, in this order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your free usage">
    Refills every day and is always spent first, so a light day of chatting can cost you nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your pro usage">
    If you subscribe to [bunny pro](/user/carrots/bunny-pro), its monthly usage goes next, before anything you have earned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your Carrot balance">
    Last of all, bunny spends the Carrots you were granted, staked for, or earned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If all three run dry, bunny tells you in chat and points you to the Mini App, so you are never left guessing.

## When you want more

- **Subscribe to bunny pro.** `$9` per month for much more usage and a sharper model. See [bunny pro](/user/carrots/bunny-pro).
- **Stake \$OS.** Lock the token on Base and earn Carrots credited daily. See [Stake OS](/user/carrots/stake-os).
- **Supply compute.** Let your GPU or Apple Silicon machine answer the network's requests and earn Carrots per job. See [Contribute compute](/user/compute/overview).

## `$OS`, the network's token

`$OS` is the bunnyOS token, held on the Base network. Staking it is a way of turning a token holding into an ongoing stream of Carrots. The Carrots are credited to your bunnyOS account automatically each day, with nothing to claim.

That is the whole loop. People who use bunny spend Carrots. People who supply compute or stake `$OS` earn them.

<Note>
You never need `$OS` to chat with bunny. Free usage and the starter grant cover everyday chatting, and bunny pro is bought with Telegram Stars, not tokens. `$OS` is simply another way to earn Carrots instead of buying them.
</Note>
