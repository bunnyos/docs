**Carrots** are the credit of the bunnyCompute network, **free usage** covers your everyday chatting, and **\$OS** is a token you can stake to earn more Carrots. The step-by-step how-tos live on their own pages, linked at the bottom.

## Carrots, the network's credit

Every answer bunny gives is generated by a real machine somewhere on the [bunnyCompute](/user/compute/overview) network. Carrots are the credit that pays for that work. Each answer has a small flat price in Carrots, and the machine that serves it earns Carrots for the job. The exact price depends on which model answers, and the bigger bunny pro model costs more per answer than the free one.

## Your usage covers everyday chatting

Answers are covered by two kinds of usage before your Carrot balance is spent.

- **Free usage**, which every account gets and which refills every single day.
- **Pro usage**, a much bigger monthly helping, if you subscribe to [bunny pro](/user/carrots/bunny-pro).

The **Usage** screen in the [Mini App](/user/getting-started/mini-app) shows where you stand as a simple percentage.

## The order things get spent

Answers draw from your usage before your balance, in this order.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your free usage">
    Refills every day and is always spent first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your pro usage">
    If you subscribe to [bunny pro](/user/carrots/bunny-pro), its monthly usage goes next, before anything you have earned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your Carrot balance">
    Once both kinds of usage are spent, bunny draws on the Carrots you have staked for or earned.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If all three run dry, bunny tells you in chat and points you to the Mini App, so you are never left guessing.

## When you want more

- **Subscribe to bunny pro.** `$9` per month for much more usage and a sharper model. See [bunny pro](/user/carrots/bunny-pro).
- **Stake \$OS.** Lock the token on Base and earn Carrots credited daily. See [Stake OS](/user/carrots/stake-os).
- **Supply compute.** Let your GPU or Apple Silicon machine answer the network's requests and earn Carrots per job. See [Contribute compute](/user/compute/overview).

## `$OS`, the network's token

`$OS` is the bunnyOS token, held on the Base network. Staking it is a way of turning a token holding into an ongoing stream of Carrots. The Carrots are credited to your bunnyOS account automatically each day, with nothing to claim.

That is the whole loop. People who use bunny spend Carrots. People who supply compute or stake `$OS` earn them.

<Note>
You never need `$OS` to chat with bunny. Free usage covers everyday chatting, and bunny pro is bought with Telegram Stars, not tokens. `$OS` is simply another way to earn Carrots instead of buying them.
</Note>
