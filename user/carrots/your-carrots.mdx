Chatting draws on your free usage first and your Carrot balance after. This page shows you where to look to see what you have, what you have spent, and how much of today's usage is left.

## Checking your balance

There are two ways, one quick and one detailed.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask in chat">
    Send `/carrots` to bunny and it replies with your balance. This is the fastest check.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the Mini App">
    Open the [Mini App](/user/getting-started/mini-app) and choose **Carrots Balance** under **Manage**. The screen leads with your balance in large type. Everything below it is your history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
Your balance belongs to your Telegram account. `/carrots` in chat and the Mini App always show the same number.
</Note>

## Your history

Under the balance is every Carrot movement on your account, newest first. Entries are grouped by day under headers like `Today` and `Yesterday`. Each row shows what happened and when on the left, and the amount on the right. Amounts you spent are negative. Amounts you gained carry a `+`.

Rows load 50 at a time. Tap **Show more** at the bottom to keep going back.

| Entry | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Inference | An answer you asked for, in chat or through the API |
| Worker earning | Carrots your machine earned by answering a job for the network |
| Refund | Carrots returned to your balance |
| Starter grant | The one-time batch of Carrots every new account receives |
| Stake | Your daily reward for `$OS` you have staked |
| Adjustment | A correction to your balance |

## The Usage screen

Chatting rarely touches your balance. Your free usage is spent first, and it refills every day. See [Carrots and OS explained](/user/carrots/explained) for the full spending order.

Tap **Usage** under **Manage** to see where you stand.

- **FREE DAILY USAGE** shows a **Daily allowance** row with the percentage used, a progress bar, and how long until it resets (for example `Resets in 5h`).
- **SUBSCRIPTION** shows a **Pro quota** row the same way if you are on [bunny pro](/user/carrots/bunny-pro), along with your renewal date. If you are not subscribed, it invites you to get pro from the main menu instead.

<Tip>
Usage is always shown as a percentage. Exact Carrot amounts appear only on the **Carrots Balance** screen, in your balance and history.
</Tip>

## When you run out

You do not have to watch the meter. If a message lands with nothing left to spend, bunny tells you right there in chat. The message arrives with two buttons, one to open the Mini App and one to subscribe to [bunny pro](/user/carrots/bunny-pro). For every way to earn more, see [Carrots and OS explained](/user/carrots/explained).
